Download **komori-0.8.14.apk** from Assets below. Android 8.0 or newer. Install over your existing app to preserve your Library, progress and downloads.

## Komori 0.8.14

- Read WeebCentral chapter titles such as "File 34" as numeric chapter numbers, so titles that use file numbering display oldest-first, open the actual first chapter, and keep previous/next navigation in numeric order.
- Preserve existing "Chapter N" and "Ch. N" parsing for other sources, including decimal releases such as "File 27.2", and keep Smart Chapters grouping unchanged.
- Add source parsing coverage that verifies File chapters are ordered 2, 2.5 and 10 rather than by their encoded chapter IDs.
